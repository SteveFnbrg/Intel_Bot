## 1. Positioning Strategy (read this first)

Before the copy, the one decision that shapes everything: **what is this profile *for*?** The writing below is tuned for **thought leadership + business development** — i.e. you look like the person who sees the whole electric/hydrogen aviation board and knows the players. If your real goal is an active job search, tighten the "About" toward outcomes and add a quiet "open to work" signal.

Your differentiator is not "I work in aerospace." It's:

- **You synthesize signal, not noise.** You built an automated intelligence pipeline that filters an entire industry's news down to strategic impact. Very few strategy people *build the tool.*
- **You think in frameworks.** The "Three Games" lens is a genuine intellectual asset — it's a memorable, ownable point of view. Lead with it.
- **You are networked into the decision-makers**, not just the news.

Everything below is engineered to make those three things obvious in the first 5 seconds.
